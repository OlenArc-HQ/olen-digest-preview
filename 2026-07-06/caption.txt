TELL US IF WE MISSED SOMETHING

We follow more than 100 organizations and over 100 sources across the Slope, but we still miss things — especially events that never get a flyer. A lot of church gatherings, programs, and get-togethers just get passed along by word of mouth, so we don't always catch them. If we missed something, or your organization is putting something on, just tell us — the date, time, and place, or a page or link where you post it. Anywhere on the Arctic Slope is welcome, and we'll add it as soon as we can.

We've also been gathering community resources into the app — 127 so far, across health, housing, jobs, food, education and scholarships, and more — and it keeps growing. You can ask about any of it right there, and it's updated all the time. If your organization runs a program or has a resource that isn't public yet but you'd like people to find it, reach out and we'll help get it up so folks can find it quickly.
