TELL US WHAT TO ADD — AND WHAT TO FIX

We follow more than 100 organizations and sources across the Slope, but we still miss things — especially events that never get a flyer. If we missed something, send us the date, time, and place, or a link — anywhere on the Arctic Slope — and we'll add it.

The app also carries 127+ community resources — health, housing, jobs, food, scholarships and more — updated all the time. If your organization runs a program people should be able to find, reach out and we'll put it up.

Months of work have gone into this, and we want it to keep running and keep getting closer to what the community actually needs. If something's broken, confusing, or missing, tell us straight. And if you'd join us for a short one-on-one Zoom walkthrough so we can watch where the app gets stuck, we'll mail you a $25 Visa gift card as a thank-you — the earlier ones are already spoken for, and we have three left.
